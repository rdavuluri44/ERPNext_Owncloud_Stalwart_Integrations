# Approve field attendance

**Status:** in use · **Owner of this SOP:** HR · **Last reviewed:** 26 November 2026

## Purpose

Official HR attendance (and so pay) is created only when a site's approver accepts an attendance entry. The system
checks the place, the time and who may approve, but only a person can judge whether the photograph shows the named
worker at the site. Approving without looking turns every other check into paperwork.

## Who

- **Project manager** (approver of the site): approves or returns.
- **Supervisor or engineer:** corrects returned entries.
- **HR:** follows up on patterns (repeated returns, offline captures).

Nobody approves their own attendance or their own submission; the system refuses it, and another approver of the
site must do it.

## When

Every working day, for each site: after the shift's check-ins, and again before the day's attendance is needed for
reports. Entries from earlier shifts stay in the list until decided.

## Steps

1. Open the field team page and choose **Approvals**, then the site.
2. For each entry, open **Review evidence** and check:
   - the **photograph** shows the named worker, live at the site (not a picture of a picture, not another person);
   - the **shift date and shift** are right;
   - the **work allocation** (operating with which machine, or standby) is right for machine workers;
   - an entry marked **Captured offline** carries the phone's clock, not the server's: look at it more carefully,
     and compare the time with what you know of the day;
   - an entry recorded by an engineer shows **Verified by** and the method.
3. If it is right, **Approve**. This creates the HR attendance.
4. If anything is wrong or unclear, write the reason in **Review remarks** and choose **Return for correction**.
   The supervisor is told and corrects it. Do not approve "to fix later".
5. Use **Approve selected** only for entries whose evidence you have opened. Bulk approval is for speed, not for
   skipping the look.
6. **Self check-ins** by staff with the app appear in the same list; check them the same way.
7. When face attendance is in use (P95): first clear the **Face queue** tab. There, pick the worker in each
   photograph the system could not settle, or reject it with a reason; recorded captures then appear in Approvals.
   A rejected capture keeps its photograph for 30 days, then only the record and your reason remain.

## Checks

- No entry of the day is left waiting without a reason.
- Every returned entry has a reason the supervisor can act on.

## Record

The system records who approved or returned each entry, when, and the remarks, and links the HR attendance to its
evidence. Nothing else needs to be written down.

## When it goes wrong

- **An approved entry turns out to be wrong:** do not delete or edit the HR attendance directly. Tell HR; the
  correction and cancellation rules are not agreed yet (inventory C6, P24).
- **You are the only approver and the entry is your own submission:** another approver must be added to the site
  by the admin; do not ask someone to use your account.
- **A site shows no entries although people worked:** ask the supervisor whether captures are still on the phone
  (offline) and have them open the app with a connection.
- **The same worker is photographed by two supervisors:** the second capture is refused by the system; settle with
  the supervisors who records the worker (worker assignments, inventory C2).
