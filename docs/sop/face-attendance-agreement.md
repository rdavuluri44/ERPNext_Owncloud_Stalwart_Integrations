## Face attendance at <Company> sites

This agreement is shown in English. If you prefer, choose another language and read it in that language, or ask
someone to read it to you, before you decide. If a translation and the English differ, the English version
applies.

**What we ask.** At your sites, your supervisor can mark your attendance by taking a photograph of your face. To
recognise you, we need to record your face once, from several sides (front, left, right, up, down).

**What we keep.** We do **not** keep these photographs. The system turns them into a set of numbers (a "face
template") that describes your face. The template is locked (encrypted) on <Company>'s own server. No person, not
even an administrator, can open it or see your face from it. When your supervisor takes your attendance photo, the
system compares it only with the people of your team on that site and shift, and tells the supervisor your name.
The attendance photo itself is kept with your attendance record, as it is today.

**Why.** To record attendance quickly and correctly at sites, and to stop attendance being marked for someone who
is not there.

**How long.** Until you withdraw your agreement or leave <Company>. Then your face template is deleted
permanently. We keep a record that you agreed and when it ended.

**Your choice.**
- This is your choice. If you do not agree, your supervisor marks your attendance in the usual way and the project
  manager approves it.
- You can withdraw at any time by telling HR, your project manager, or your supervisor or engineer. Your template is
  deleted at once.
- You can ask HR what we hold about you, and ask us to correct it.
- Questions or complaints: <Company>'s contact for personal data, `<contact>`. You may also complain to the Data
  Protection Board of India.

**Your agreement.** By ticking "I agree", I confirm that the person enrolling me (HR, the project manager, or my
supervisor or engineer) explained these terms to me in a language I understand, that I could ask questions and
they were answered, and that I agree that my face is recorded for attendance as described above.

another)  ·  Date and time (recorded by the system)  ·  Recorded by (HR, project manager, or the worker's own
supervisor or engineer; recorded by the system)  ·  Identity document checked (recorded by the system)

  separate notice is still needed if section 7 applies.

  language of their choice, and the language they read is recorded. This fits DPDP's choice of English or a

  translation is checked by translating it back. **If a translation and the English differ, the English version

  which backs the statement up. To confirm with the lawyer: that this acknowledgement, with those records, is enough
  for informed consent when the worker cannot read the text themselves.
