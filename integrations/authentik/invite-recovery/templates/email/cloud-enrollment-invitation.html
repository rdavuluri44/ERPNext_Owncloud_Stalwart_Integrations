Set up your Cloud passkey

Your administrator has invited you to register your sign-in passkey.

Before opening the link, prepare Apple Passwords/iCloud Keychain, Bitwarden (or Vaultwarden), Google Password Manager, or Chrome on a Mac. Other providers are not accepted. A passkey saved in Chrome on a Mac stays on that Mac only.

Bitwarden:

  • Create an account at https://vault.vaultwarden.com/, preferably using the same email address as your Cloud username.
  • Install the official phone app or browser extension: https://bitwarden.com/download/
  • Before signing in, select Self-hosted and enter https://vault.vaultwarden.com. Sign in with the account you created.
  • Set up biometric unlock for convenience. Your master password may still be required when signing in again.
  • On a phone, enable Bitwarden in AutoFill/password and passkey settings.
  • Avoid installing the browser extension on shared computers.

Apple Passwords: Enable iCloud Passwords & Keychain syncing on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Have your device passcode or Touch ID/Face ID ready.
