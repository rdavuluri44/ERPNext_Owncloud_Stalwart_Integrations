Get ready before opening the link

Use Apple Passwords/iCloud Keychain, Bitwarden (or Vaultwarden), Google Password Manager or Chrome on a Mac to save your sign-in passkey. Other providers are not accepted. A passkey saved in Chrome on a Mac stays on that Mac only.

Bitwarden:

  • Create an account at vault.vaultwarden.com, preferably using the same email address as your Cloud username.
  • Install the official phone app or browser extension.
  • Before signing in to the installed app or extension, change the server to Self-hosted and enter https://vault.vaultwarden.com. Sign in using the account you just created.
  • Set up biometric unlock for convenient unlocking. You may still need your master password when signing in again.
  • On a phone, enable Bitwarden in your device's AutoFill/password and passkey settings.
  • Avoid installing the Bitwarden browser extension on shared computers.

Using Apple Passwords: on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, enable iCloud Passwords & Keychain syncing in your Apple Account's iCloud settings. Have your device passcode or Touch ID/Face ID ready.

Apple setup guide

Register your passkey

  1. Once your password manager is ready, open the invitation button below.
  2. Check that the registration page is on sso.example.com. Complete the separate email verification sent to this same email address.
  3. When asked to create a passkey, choose Bitwarden, Apple Passwords/iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager or Chrome on this Mac as the save location.
  4. Confirm with your fingerprint, face or device PIN when prompted, and finish the on-screen steps. Keep access to the password manager for future sign-ins.

If neither approved provider appears, stop and contact your administrator. Do not choose a different provider. Never send anyone your vault master password, device PIN or passkey.
